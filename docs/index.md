faqs:
  - question: Is Reminder free?
    answer: Yes. The app itself and the British Shorthair companion are free. Additional pets are optional in-app purchases — $3.99 each, or $19.99 for the All Pets Pack. Reminder features never require a purchase.
  - question: Do I need an account or an internet connection?
    answer: No. Reminders, notes, stats and pet animations all work offline. An internet connection is only needed to download a newly purchased pet pack.
  - question: Which macOS versions are supported?
    answer: Reminder supports macOS 12 Monterey and later, on both Apple Silicon (M-series) and Intel Macs.
  - question: Which languages does the app speak?
    answer: 25 interface languages, including English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, Spanish and French. Light and dark themes follow your system.
  - question: Is my data private?
    answer: Everything is stored locally on your Mac. There are no analytics SDKs, no ads, no tracking and no cloud sync. In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple.
