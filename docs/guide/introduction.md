# Introduction

## About Reminder

**Reminder** is a calm, local-first reminder app for macOS — with a small desktop companion that notices when you follow through. Set a reminder, finish it, and watch your pet grow with your streak.

Version **2.0.0** is a complete rebuild: a native Rust + [GPUI](https://gpui.rs) app with no WebView and no JavaScript runtime. It starts in about a second and the whole bundle is roughly 24 MB.

## What's in 2.0.0

- **A rebuilt task window** — write it, schedule it and check it off in one place, without jumping back to the list
- **Three-column workspace** — smart lists on the left, tasks in the middle, details on the right; the middle column can be dragged to the width you like
- **Markdown notes** — block-level editing for task descriptions and standalone notes: headings, lists, to-dos, quotes, code and dividers, still saved as Markdown
- **Native reminders** — one-off and repeating rules, snooze, system notifications, sounds, global shortcuts and a persistent menu-bar tray
- **A desktop pet** — a transparent floating window with sprite animations; it walks out when a reminder fires, follows your cursor, and responds to clicks
- **Pet store** — preview, purchase and restore illustrated companions through StoreKit 2
- **Statistics & achievements** — completion trends, streak days and 11 built-in achievements
- **25 interface languages**, light and dark themes, accent colours and launch-at-login

## Designed to Stay Out of the Way

No feeds, no ads, no notifications you didn't ask for. There is no account and no sign-in: your reminders, notes and pet data stay on your Mac. App Sandbox is enabled and the app only touches files you pick yourself.

## System Requirements

- **macOS**: 12 Monterey or later
- **CPU**: Apple Silicon (M-series) or Intel (64-bit)
- **Storage**: about 30 MB for the app, plus optional pet packs
