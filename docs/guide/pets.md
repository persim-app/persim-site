# Pets & In-App Purchases

Reminder ships with one free companion. Extra illustrated pets are optional, cosmetic in-app purchases — every reminder feature works without them.

## The Companions

| Pet | Price | Notes |

| Shiba Inu | $3.99 | Featured — lively, independent Shiba Inu |
| **All Pets Pack** | **$19.99** | Unlocks every premium pet in the store |

Prices shown are USD reference prices. StoreKit converts them to your local currency at purchase time.

Each pet has its own sprite animations, moods and sound effects. Featured pets are marked with a badge in the store.

## Buying a Pet

1. Open **Pet Store** from the tray menu or the Pets section of Settings
2. Select a pet and preview its animations
3. Click **Buy** and confirm with the App Store
4. The pack downloads, is verified (SHA-256) and installs locally — no account needed
5. Select the pet to make it your active companion

Tap **Restore Purchases** at any time to unlock pets you already bought on another Mac signed in with the same Apple ID.

## Import & Export

You can import a `.catpet` pack you already own, and export your installed pets so a companion can move with you. Imported packs are checked before they are installed.

## Manage Pets

In **Settings → Pet** you can preview the active companion, switch pets, adjust behaviour and appearance, toggle interaction sounds, and pause animation while the screen is locked.

## Common Questions

- **Does buying a pet unlock any reminder feature?** No. Purchases are cosmetic and support development.
- **Can I use a purchased pet offline?** Yes. Once downloaded, packs are stored on your Mac.
- **Where are packs stored?** Inside the app's local data folder; see [Privacy Choices](./privacy-choices.md).

Need help with a purchase? See [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md).
