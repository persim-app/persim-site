# Customization

Reminder follows your system where it can, and stays out of the way where it can't. Open **Settings** with `Cmd+,` or from the tray menu.

## General

- **Display Language** — 25 interface languages; the change applies immediately to every open window
- **Accent Colour** — several presets that apply to buttons, selection and the editor
- **Launch at Startup** — start Reminder when you log in
- **Always Show Tray Icon** — keep the menu-bar icon visible even with all windows closed
- **Data folder / Log folder** — open the local folders directly
- **Restart Onboarding** — replay the first-run guide

## Appearance

Light and dark themes follow your macOS setting by default, or you can pin one. The floating pet window adapts with the rest of the app.

## Reminders

- Default reminder time and snooze length
- Notification sound, pet sounds and interaction sounds
- Global shortcuts for "new reminder" and "show/hide pet"
- Pause all reminders from the tray menu

## Pet

- Preview and switch the active companion
- Behaviour: idle actions, movement, interaction response
- Appearance: size and animation speed
- Pause animation while the screen is locked
- Open the pet store, import a pack or restore purchases

## Tray Menu

The menu-bar icon shows today's count, completed count and the next reminder, plus quick actions for new reminder, pause/resume, pet visibility, settings and quit.

See [Pets & IAP](./pets.md) for the store, and [Usage](./usage.md) for keyboard shortcuts.
